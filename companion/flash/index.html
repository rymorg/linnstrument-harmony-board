Install the Harmony Board

Install or update the Harmony Board firmware on a LinnStrument 200, straight from this page. It backs up your settings, calibration, sequencer projects and Harmony Board data first, writes the new firmware, checks every byte, and puts your settings back. Nothing to download or install.

This browser can't talk to the LinnStrument

The flasher needs the Web Serial API: use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop computer (macOS, Windows, Linux or ChromeOS). Safari, Firefox and phones can't do it.

Or use the downloadable flasher instead: see INSTALL.md.

Use at your own risk

The Harmony Board is unofficial, experimental firmware, provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It isn't made, tested or supported by Roger Linn Design. If flashing is interrupted, the LinnStrument won't work until it's flashed again; that's almost always recoverable (see If something goes wrong).

  • Plug the LinnStrument straight into the computer, not into a hub, and keep a laptop on its charger.
  • The lights go dark for a minute or two while it flashes. That's expected. Don't unplug it, don't touch it, and keep this tab open and in front until it says Done.

Pick the LinnStrument again

The LinnStrument restarted and reconnected, and Chrome needs you to choose it once more to carry on. Nothing is lost. Don't unplug it.

1Back up the LinnStrument

  1. Plug in the LinnStrument and press Global Settings (the bottom button on the left edge).
  2. Briefly press OS Update once: column 16, counting from the control buttons, third pad from the bottom, so its light turns on. Use one finger and don't touch the bottom pad of that column: pressing both together resets the settings.
  3. Click the button and choose LinnStrument in the list. Reading the settings changes nothing on the LinnStrument.

2Save the backup file

Keep this file until the update is done: if anything goes wrong, it brings back your settings and calibration. The flasher also keeps a copy in this browser.

1Choose your backup

The .lsbackup file from an earlier run (this flasher or the downloadable one, which keeps them in the linnstrument-backups folder in your home folder).

2Restore

For a LinnStrument that's waiting in serial mode: one that was just flashed and hasn't been calibrated yet, or one with OS Update turned on. Projects and Harmony Board data go first, settings last; then it leaves serial mode.

3Firmware

Looking up the latest release…

Only for your own builds: a .bin, optionally with its .elf to check the settings layout.

4Erase and install

Without a backup, the LinnStrument's settings, calibration and Harmony Board data are lost. Use this to recover a LinnStrument that can't answer: one in Firmware Upgrade mode (unplug it, hold Global Settings and plug it back in: it shows FWUP in red), or one with dark lights after an interrupted flash. Afterwards you calibrate it again.

The LinnStrument shows a red WAIT, then its lights go out while it's erased, written and checked (about two minutes). Then it restarts and gets its settings back, and shows a green OK.

  • Show WAIT and start the bootloader
  • Erase and write the firmware
  • Check every byte and start the firmware
  • Restore settings, projects and Harmony Board data

If something goes wrong

    
The LinnStrument's processor has a recovery bootloader in read-only memory that flashing can't erase, so a LinnStrument with dark lights can still take new firmware. In order:

    
    
      
  1. Leave it plugged in and flash again from this page: choose Recover: flash with a saved backup and your backup file. Without one, choose Recover: flash without a backup (you'll calibrate afterwards).
    2. 
      
  2. Use Firmware Upgrade mode. Unplug the LinnStrument, hold Global Settings and plug it back in: it shows FWUP in red. Then use Recover: flash without a backup, or with your backup.
    3. 
      
  3. Use the official LinnStrument Updater from rogerlinndesign.com, which installs the stock firmware. Roger Linn Design's FAQ says a LinnStrument with no lights "will still accept an OS update".
    4. 
      
  4. If none of that works, the hardware may need repair. Roger Linn Design offers repair options for LinnStrument hardware; tell them it was running unofficial firmware, and don't ask them for help with the Harmony Board itself.
